# Ideation matrix — pick one, kill two

Generate exactly 3 candidate ideas, then score each 1-5 on the four axes below. Highest total wins; no ties, no "we'll combine them".

| Axis | Question to ask | Why it wins |
|---|---|---|
| Rubric fit | Does it visibly hit the top-weighted criteria? | 40% of the score is whatever judges weight most. |
| Demoability | Can a stranger see it working in 20 seconds? | "Live demo" is the highest-leverage criterion. |
| Feasibility | Is the riskiest part provable in the spike block? | An unspiked assumption is a time bomb at hour 30. |
| Sponsor fit | Does it use a sponsor's API the way they hoped? | Sponsor prizes are a smaller field. |

## Tie-breakers

- Prefer the idea where the demo works **offline** (no API dependency, no live data).
- Prefer the idea your team can explain in one sentence to a non-technical judge.
- Prefer the one with the lowest external-service count.

## Anti-patterns (instantly disqualified)

- "We'll build a platform" — platforms need users you don't have.
- "The AI will figure it out" — unspiked LLM behavior is the #1 demo failure mode.
- Two ideas merged — the union is never scoped, it's just two half-projects.
