# In-person packing list

## Power (the scarce resource)

- [ ] **Power strip** — outlets are scarce; bringing one makes you instantly popular AND guarantees your spot
- [ ] Laptop charger + phone charger + cables (one spare)
- [ ] Power bank (booth judging happens away from walls)

## Sleep kit

- [ ] Earplugs + eye mask (venues don't dim for you)
- [ ] Hoodie/blanket — venue AC is always arctic
- [ ] Change of clothes + toothbrush — looking alive at the demo is worth points

## Demo kit

- [ ] HDMI/USB-C adapters (judge screens vary)
- [ ] Mouse (trackpad demos look amateur)
- [ ] Phone + hotspot plan tested
- [ ] Sticky notes for teammate signals ("DEMO IN 10" beats shouting)

## Human

- [ ] ID (venue entry + sponsor swag)
- [ ] Water bottle, snacks — venue food lines eat 40 minutes
- [ ] Any medication; don't rely on finding a pharmacy mid-event
